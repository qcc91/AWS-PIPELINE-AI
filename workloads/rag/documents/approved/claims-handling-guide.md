## First notice of loss

Record the claim number, policy number, incident date, loss description, and
the claimant's preferred contact channel. A complete first notice is required
before an adjuster assigns an initial reserve.

## Waiting period

For the demonstration product, eligible accidental-damage claims have a
fourteen-calendar-day waiting period from policy commencement. The policy
schedule controls if it contains a longer period.

## Evidence and decision

Request only evidence relevant to the covered loss. The adjuster records a
coverage decision, rationale, and decision date. Escalate suspected fraud or
conflicts of interest to the claims manager.
