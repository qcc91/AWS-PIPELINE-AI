# Standard accident cover product terms (synthetic V1 document)

Document ID: `standard-accident-cover`
Version: `1.0`
Source: `synthetic-product-team`
Classification: `internal`
Effective date: `2026-01-01`

The standard accident cover reimburses approved repair costs up to the limit
shown on the policy schedule, less the applicable excess. It does not cover
deliberate loss, wear and tear, or an event outside the policy period.

Customers may request a plain-language explanation of a coverage decision.
The explanation must identify the relevant policy section and include the
claim contact path; it must not expose internal scoring or another customer's
information.
